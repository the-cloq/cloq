Delivery

We offer free shipping, worldwide.

Delivery: Usually 3-6 working days after ship-out.

Our delivery courier is UPS. All orders are insured by CLOQ during shipment.


Please note:
  • Shipping to China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan is currently not possible.
  • Deliveries may be delayed in certain geographical areas or if your order requires special validation (e.g. custom related matters).
  • Orders are shipped in a solid packaging box complete with a carrier’s handle.
  • When your order has been shipped from our studio in Buckinghamshire, England, you will be sent a shipping confirmation with a UPS tracking number.
  • Upon delivery of the package, you shall be notified by UPS via text message, phone call or email. In any case where you are not notified by UPS upon delivery of your package, the delivery of the package can be tracked using the provided UPS tracking number.
