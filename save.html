CLOQ is a beautifully crafted clock with a twist. A fusion of art and design technology that carries its own energy and equilibrium, comprising a stainless steel matrix clock face with 256 laser-cut letters and numbers that illuminates the time in words. Handcrafted and available in over 20 languages.

THE ARTIST

Jason Dinsdale is an award winning designer, creative director, photographer and acclaimed artist based in London. A passion for clean design, typography and simplistic, minimal art forms, his early influencers include Kasimir Malevich for his paradoxical and illogical symmetry, and Piet Mondrian - a pioneer of abstract art, were he reached a point where his artistic vocabulary was reduced to simple geometric elements.

In the midst of Covid Lockdowns, Jase turned his weekends into a creative hobby and excerted his coding skills to take his artworks to the next level by developing and combining technology with art, to form an exquisite time piece that is both striking and practical. The result is a minimalist steel or copper square with a matrix of laser-cut typography forming the unorthodox clockface that is - CLOQ.
