ABOUT THE ARTIST

Jason Dinsdale is an award winning graphic designer, creative director, acclaimed photographer, artist and product designer based in Buckinghamshire, England. A passion for clean design, typography and simplistic, minimal art forms, his early influencers include Kasimir Malevich for his paradoxical and illogical symmetry, and Piet Mondrian - a pioneer of abstract art, were he reached a point where his artistic vocabulary was reduced to simple geometric elements.

In the midst of Covid Lockdowns, Jase turned his weekends into a new creative hobby and exerted his coding skills to take his artworks to the next level by developing and combining technology with art, to form an exquisite timepiece that is CLOQ.
