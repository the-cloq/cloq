CLOQ is a beautifully crafted clock with a twist. A fusion of art and design technology that carries its own energy and equilibrium, comprising a stainless steel matrix clock face with 256 laser-cut letters and numbers that illuminates the time in words. Handcrafted and available in over 20 languages.

The Artist

Jason Dinsdale is an award winning designer, creative director and artist based in London. A passion for clean design, typography and simplistic, minimal art forms, his early influencers include Kasimir Malevich for his paradoxical and illogical symmetry, and Piet Mondrian - a pioneer of abstract art, were he reached a point where his artistic vocabulary was reduced to simple geometric elements.

In his latest artwork, Jason introduces tech art to a minimalist stainless steel square with a matrix of laser-cut typography forming an unorthodox clock face that is - CLOQ.

Form Follows Function

CLOQ illuminates hours and minutes in words with a numeral second counter, giving a fresh outlook on time and typography. The outside ambient temperature can also be displayed.

SmArt Tech

A series of built-in sensors help maintain low energy. The web app allows you to modify the settings to suit your space

Colour

Displaying the hours, minutes and seconds in different colours

Auto Brightness

CLOQ senses the presence or absence of light, and adjusts brightness

Motion

With the motion sensor constantly alert, CLOQ reverts to standby mode if no motion is detected

WiFi

CLOQ accesses the time via WiFi, keeping millisecond precision month on month

Weather

Optional use of the free weather data service to display the temperature and ambience

App

Taking full control of all of these features couldn't be easier with the simple to use CLOQ app
