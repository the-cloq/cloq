What are cookies and how do we use them?
Cookies are tiny files that are downloaded to your computer. They improve your experience on our Site and allow us to get insights into how users interact with our Services. The next paragraph describes what information cookies gather, how we use them and why we sometimes need to store these cookies.

What types of cookies do we use?
You can find a detailed list of which cookies are set here. These cookies are required to operate our Site and process your order. For example, they ensure that your order and contact information is remembered between individual pages. In some special cases we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties. Third-party cookies include:

  • Google Analytics
    These cookies help us to understand how you use our Site. They may track things such as how much time you spend on the Site and what products you view in detail. This information helps us to improve your experience as a customer and to continue producing engaging content. For more information on Google Analytics Cookies, see the official Google Analytics page: Google Analytics Cookies
  • Hotjar
    Hotjar analytics give us a more detailed insight into what parts of our Site are used, and where customers might have difficulties understanding the content. As we sell products it is important for us to collect statistics on how many of the visitors to our Site actually make a purchase and how they navigate through the checkout process, and as such this is the kind of data these cookies will track. For more information on Hotjar cookies, see the official Hotjar page: Hotjar Cookies

How to manage cookies
You can manage/delete cookies as you wish - for details, see about cookies.org.

Removing cookies from your device
You can delete all cookies that are already on your device by clearing the browsing history of your browser. This will remove all cookies from all websites you have visited. Be aware though that you may also lose some saved information (e.g. saved login details, site preferences).

Managing site-specific cookies
For more detailed control over site-specific cookies, check the privacy and cookie settings in your preferred browser.

Blocking cookies
You can set the most modern browsers to prevent any cookies being placed on your device, but you may then have to manually adjust some preferences every time you visit a site/page. Some services and functionalities may not work properly at all (e.g. profile log-in).
